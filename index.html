How this works & iPhone tips

This app combines video frames over your chosen time. It is a simulated long exposure, not a single sensor exposure. Smooth motion preserves average brightness. Light trails keeps the brightest pixels.

Keep the phone completely still, with this app visible and the screen on. A call, screen lock, switching apps, or rotating the camera during a shot can interrupt it. If interrupted, the app keeps a partial result when possible and marks it as incomplete.

Quality is limited to the camera video stream, up to 1080p here. This is not a 48 MP or RAW camera, cannot recover stars that the video camera cannot see, and does not replace Night mode. Rapid lights may leave gaps between video frames. Safari controls the camera's focus and sensor exposure automatically.

In Safari: Share > Add to Home Screen > Add. No App Store, payment, or developer account is needed. Open once online so the app can cache itself for offline use. Browser storage can be cleared by iOS, so open it before heading somewhere without reception.

No analytics, uploads, or remote image processing. GitHub serves the app files. Camera frames and exported photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Completed photos and brightness changes are saved automatically to this browser's local gallery. Check the saved confirmation before leaving. Local browser storage is not a permanent backup, and Safari and a Home Screen app may have separate storage. Save important shots to Photos or Files.
